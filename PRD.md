1.INTRODUCTION

Campus maintenance is an important issue in a university environment.
Maintenance is an important part of every university because students depend on different facilities on campus for their daily activities. However, maintenance problems are something I have personally experienced, and I have also noticed that other students face similar challenges.

Some of these problems involve electricity, plumbing, internet connection, faulty doors and air conditioning. Students report maintenance problems to the school authorities, but from our observations, there has not been much improvement in some of these situations.

This made our team interested in finding a better way to address the problem. We decided to investigate how students experience maintenance issues and how they currently report them. Our aim is to propose a web-based system that will make it easier for students to report maintenance problems and check the progress of their complaints.

2.BACKGROUND

Students use different facilities on campus every day.Babcock University has different facilities that students use for academic and everyday activities. These facilities need proper maintenance so that students can use them without unnecessary difficulties.

However, maintenance issues such as poor internet connection, plumbing problems, electrical faults, faulty doors and air conditioning can affect students’ comfort and daily activities.

From our preliminary research, we discovered that both respondents frequently experience maintenance problems. One respondent had previously reported a problem through hostel management, while the other had never reported one.

Our team believes that there is a need to investigate how maintenance complaints are currently handled and whether a web-based reporting system could make the process easier for students and the people responsible for handling maintenance requests.


3.PROBLEM IDENTIFICATION

Some maintenance problems can affect students' daily activities.The problem our team wants to address is the difficulty associated with reporting and following up on maintenance issues within the university.

Students experience different maintenance problems, including electrical faults, plumbing issues, poor internet connection, faulty doors and air conditioning problems.

From our Google Forms survey, both respondents indicated that they frequently experience maintenance problems. One respondent had reported a problem through hostel management and stated that it took more than a week to resolve. The other respondent had never reported a maintenance problem.

Both respondents also indicated that they would prefer an online platform for reporting maintenance issues.

Based on these findings, our team wants to investigate how a web-based system could make it easier to submit maintenance complaints and follow their progress.



4.RESEARCH METHOLOGY

To understand the maintenance problems experienced by students, our team carried out preliminary research using Google Forms.

We created a questionnaire to collect information about the types of maintenance problems students experience, how frequently they experience them, their previous reporting experiences and their opinions about an online maintenance reporting platform.

We received two responses to the questionnaire. We reviewed the answers to identify common problems and understand what the respondents think about the proposed solution.

Apart from the questionnaire, one member of our team has personally experienced maintenance problems. This also contributed to our interest in the project.

No formal interviews have been conducted so far. Therefore, our findings are based on the two survey responses and the personal experience mentioned above.

5.RESEARCH FINDINGS

After reviewing the two responses collected through Google Forms, our team identified the following findings:

1.Both respondents indicated that they frequently experience maintenance problems.
2.The maintenance problems selected included internet/network issues, plumbing, electrical faults, faulty doors and air conditioning.
3.One respondent had previously reported a maintenance problem, while the other had never reported one.
4.The respondent who had reported a problem used hostel management as the reporting channel.
5.The respondent indicated that the problem took more than a week to be resolved.
6.Both respondents said that maintenance problems moderately affect their daily activities.
7.Both respondents selected slow administrative processes as a possible reason for delays in resolving maintenance problems.
8.Both respondents expressed interest in using an online platform to report maintenance problems.

5.1 Satisfaction Rating

The respondents gave the following satisfaction ratings:

.First respondent: 2 out of 5.
.Second respondent: 3 out of 5.

The average satisfaction rating was 2.5 out of 5.

This suggests that the respondents were not highly satisfied with their maintenance experiences.

5.2 What We Learned

From the responses, we learned that maintenance problems are experienced by the respondents and that both are interested in having an online reporting option.

These findings encouraged our team to explore a system that would allow users to submit maintenance complaints and monitor their progress.


6.LIMITATIONS OF OUR RESEACRCH

Although our team was able to collect useful information through the Google Forms questionnaire, there are some limitations to our research.

1.We received only two responses, which is not enough to represent the experiences of the entire university community.
2.We have not conducted formal interviews with students.
3.We have not interviewed maintenance personnel or school administrators.
4.We do not yet have complete information about how maintenance complaints are reported and handled across the university.
5.We have not confirmed the reasons why some maintenance problems take a long time to resolve.

Because of these limitations, our findings should be treated as preliminary. We understand that more research is needed to have a better understanding of the problem and the people involved.



7.EXISTING REPORTING PROCESS
rom the responses collected, we found that one respondent had previously reported a maintenance problem through hostel management, while the other respondent had never reported a maintenance problem.

This shows that hostel management was used as a reporting channel by at least one respondent. However, our research has not established the complete process used to report maintenance problems in other parts of the university.

We still need to find out who receives maintenance complaints, how the complaints are recorded, how they are assigned and how students are informed about the progress of their complaints.

Our proposed system will therefore be designed with these unanswered questions in mind.


8.PEOPLE INVOLVED (Stakeholder Analysis)

There are different groups of people who may be involved in the proposed Campus Maintenance Reporting and Tracking System.

1.Students and Campus Users

Students and other campus users are the main intended users of the system. They will be able to report maintenance problems, provide information about affected facilities and check the progress of their complaints.

2.School Administrators

Administrators are expected to review submitted complaints and help coordinate how maintenance requests are handled. Their exact responsibilities will need to be confirmed through further research.

3.Maintenance Personnel

Maintenance personnel may be responsible for attending to reported problems. Through the proposed system, they could receive assigned requests and update their progress.

The team will need to investigate the actual responsibilities of these groups before finalising the system’s workflow.



9.POSSIBLE CAUSES OF THE PROBLEM

From our survey, both respondents selected slow administrative processes as a possible reason why maintenance problems take time to be resolved.

However, our team has not yet confirmed whether this is the actual cause of delays within the university.

Other possible factors that need further investigation include how complaints are received, how maintenance tasks are assigned and the availability of the resources needed to carry out repairs.

Understanding these factors will help us design a system that addresses the actual challenges rather than making assumptions.



10.PROPOSED SOLUTION

Our team proposes to develop a web-based Campus Maintenance Reporting and Tracking System for Babcock University.

The purpose of the system is to provide students and other authorised campus users with an easier way to report maintenance problems and follow the progress of their complaints.

Users will be able to submit details about the problems they experience, select the type of maintenance issue and identify the affected location. They will also have the option to upload pictures to explain the problem better.

The system will provide an administrative dashboard where submitted complaints can be viewed and managed. Maintenance personnel will also be able to receive assigned requests and update their progress.

We hope that the proposed system will make maintenance reporting more organised and improve the visibility of complaints from the time they are submitted until they are resolved.



11.GOALS AND OBJECTIVES

The main goal of this project is to make reporting and tracking maintenance problems easier across Babcock University.

The objectives are:

1.To provide an online platform for reporting maintenance problems.
2.To allow users to explain the problems they experience.
3.To help users identify the location of maintenance problems.
4.To allow users to monitor the progress of their complaints.
5.To provide administrators with a way to view and manage submitted complaints.
6.To support communication between users, administrators and maintenance personnel.
7.To keep records of reported maintenance problems and their progress.



12.USER REQUIREMENTS

The proposed system should provide the following functions to its users:

1.Users should be able to create an account and log in.
2.Students should be able to submit maintenance complaints.
3.Users should be able to select the category of the problem.
4.Users should be able to provide the location of the affected facility.
5.Users should be able to describe the problem clearly.
6.Users should be able to attach pictures of the maintenance problem.
7.Users should be able to view their submitted complaints.
8.Users should be able to check the progress of their complaints.
9.Administrators should be able to view submitted complaints.
10.Administrators should be able to assign maintenance requests to the appropriate personnel.
11.Maintenance personnel should be able to update the progress of assigned tasks.


13.FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS

The following are the main functions expected from the proposed system.
1.User Registration and Login
The system should allow users to create accounts and log in securely.

2.Submit Maintenance Complaints
The system should allow users to submit complaints about maintenance problems on campus.

3.Select Maintenance Category
Users should be able to select the type of maintenance problem they want to report, such as plumbing, electrical faults, internet problems or air conditioning.

4.Provide Location Details
Users should be able to identify the building or facility where the problem occurred.

5.Upload Pictures
The system should allow users to attach pictures to help explain the maintenance problem.

6.Track Complaints
Users should be able to view the status of their submitted complaints.

7.Manage Complaints
Administrators should be able to view, review and manage maintenance complaints.

8.Assign Maintenance Requests
Authorised administrators should be able to assign maintenance requests to relevant personnel.

9.Update Request Status
Maintenance personnel should be able to update the progress of assigned requests.

10.View Request History
The system should maintain records of maintenance requests and their updates.




Acceptance Criteria

The Campus Maintenance Reporting and Tracking System will be considered acceptable when the following conditions are met:

1.User Registration and Login: Students should be able to register and securely log in to the system.
2.Maintenance Complaint Submission: Students should be able to submit maintenance complaints concerning issues such as faulty electrical appliances, leaking pipes, damaged hostel facilities, and other campus-related problems.
3.Complaint Details: Students should be able to provide a clear description of the problem and specify its location.
4.Complaint Tracking: Students should be able to view the status of their submitted complaints.
5.Status Updates: The system should allow authorized maintenance personnel or administrators to update complaint statuses to Pending, In Progress, or Resolved.
6.Complaint History: Students should be able to view previously submitted complaints and their resolutions.
7.Administrative Access: Administrators should be able to view and manage complaints submitted by students.
8.Successful Resolution: A complaint should only be marked as Resolved after the reported maintenance issue has been addressed.
9.User-Friendly Interface: The system should be simple, accessible, and easy for students and administrators to navigate.
10.Reliable Information: The system should accurately store submitted complaints and display updated information to users.



14.NON-FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
The proposed system should meet the following requirements:
1.Usability
The system should have a simple interface that students and other users can understand and use without difficulty.

2.Security
The system should protect users’ account information and prevent unauthorised people from accessing administrative functions.

3.Performance
The system should respond within a reasonable time when users submit complaints or access their accounts.

4.Reliability
The system should store submitted complaints correctly and ensure that request updates are recorded.

5.Accessibility
The web application should be accessible through common web browsers on mobile phones, tablets and computers.

6.Maintainability
The system should be organised in a way that allows future improvements and additional features to be introduced.

17.PROPOSED REQUEST STATUS

The system will use different statuses to show users the progress of their maintenance complaints.

The proposed statuses are:
1.Submitted: The complaint has been successfully sent.
2.Under Review: The complaint is being examined by an administrator.
3.Assigned: The complaint has been assigned to maintenance personnel.
4.In Progress: Work on the maintenance problem has started.
5.Resolved: The reported problem has been fixed.
6.Closed: The maintenance request has been completed and closed.

These statuses are proposed for the new system and may be adjusted after further research.



18. POSSIBLE CHALLENGES AND RISKS

While developing and using the proposed system, there may be some challenges.
1.Internet Access: Users will need an internet connection to access the web application.
2.User Adoption: Some students may prefer to continue using existing reporting methods.
3.Delayed Responses: Maintenance requests may still take time to resolve if they are not attended to promptly.
4.Security: User information must be protected from unauthorised access.
5.Incomplete Information: Some users may submit complaints without providing enough details about the problem.
6.Administrative Participation: The system will depend on authorised personnel reviewing and updating submitted requests.

The team will consider these challenges when planning the system.




19. Conclusion

The Campus Maintenance Reporting and Tracking System is a proposed solution aimed at improving how maintenance problems are reported and monitored within Babcock University.

Our preliminary research showed that the two respondents frequently experience maintenance problems and that both expressed interest in using an online platform to report them.

Through this project, our team hopes to provide a more organised way for students and other campus users to submit maintenance complaints and follow their progress.

We also recognise that further research is needed to understand the existing maintenance process and the responsibilities of the people involved.

The proposed system will initially focus on the basic features needed for reporting, managing and tracking maintenance requests, with room for future improvements.
